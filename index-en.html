Ultrasonic Cleaning

Deep cleaning for those who want to go further.

Ultrasonic waves help loosen dirt accumulated in the grooves of vinyl records, reaching areas that conventional cleaning cannot always reach.

Our process uses impurity-free water produced through reverse osmosis, antifungal treatment and careful finishing. Each record also receives a new inner sleeve, completing the cleaning and preservation process.

Each session lasts between 30 and 60 minutes, depending on the assessment of each batch. Our machine can clean up to 15 records at a time, ensuring efficient service for larger batches from collectors and retailers.

Giro Groove DD

Direct Drive turntable developed by Giro Limpo.

The Giro Groove project was born from a simple need: to create affordable turntables for people with limited budgets that would not damage records through excessive stylus tracking force.

From the beginning, the goal was to develop simple and lightweight projects, produced through 3D printing, while still offering excellent audio quality. For this, we use cost-effective cartridges that are easy to find and replace.

The tonearm was designed to be simple to use and require no complex adjustments. The stylus tracking force is 2.5 grams, an ideal weight for achieving good audio quality without applying excessive pressure to the record and therefore avoiding damage to the vinyl.

After many 3D-printed Direct Drive prototypes and a Belt Drive model, which eventually became a commercial DIY product and is now available here as an open-source project, we reached a new stage of development.

From this evolution, the Giro Groove DD was born.

It combines the simplicity and lightness of the tonearm from previous projects with the durability and practicality of a Direct Drive motor, without the need for a belt.

In addition to the Direct Drive system, the Giro Groove DD features a Line output, allowing you to connect it directly to a speaker you already have at home, without the need for additional equipment for this connection.

A simple, affordable turntable designed for those who want to listen to their records without complications.

The Giro Groove DD can be ordered via WhatsApp.

Giro Groove DIY

DIY turntable project with files available free of charge.

After many prototypes, tests and improvements, the Giro Groove belt-drive turntable reached a version that was produced and had several units sold.

Now, this project is being made freely available to anyone who wants to build their own turntable at home, for personal and non-commercial use.

As an open-source project, you can customize your Giro Groove however you like: use stickers, combine colors, experiment with different filaments and create a version that reflects your personality.

If you build one, share the result on social media and tag Giro Limpo. We would love to see the different versions of the project out there. Whenever possible, please give proper credit to Giro Limpo.
