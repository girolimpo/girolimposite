Products

How should I use Limpa Discos?

Limpa Discos should be applied to the surface of the vinyl according to the usage instructions. Use a suitable microfiber cloth to remove dirt and finish cleaning the record.

Can Limpa Discos be used on any vinyl record?

The product was developed for cleaning vinyl records. Before applying it, we recommend checking the condition of the record and following the usage instructions carefully.

What is Veda Selo?

Veda Selo is an accessory developed to help protect record labels during the cleaning process.

Ultrasonic Cleaning

How many records can I send for cleaning?

Giro Limpo provides vinyl record cleaning in batches. The minimum quantity and service conditions can be checked directly via WhatsApp.

Do you clean large batches of records?

Yes. The service can accommodate collectors and record stores that need to clean batches of different sizes.

How does ultrasonic cleaning work?

The process uses ultrasonic waves to help remove dirt accumulated in the grooves of the record. The process includes preparation, cleaning and finishing stages.

Giro Groove DD

Is the Giro Groove DD available for purchase?

The Giro Groove DD can be produced to order. To check availability, pricing and purchasing conditions, contact Giro Limpo via WhatsApp.

Does the Giro Groove DD require a phono preamp?

The Giro Groove DD has a Line output, allowing it to be connected directly to equipment compatible with this type of signal.
